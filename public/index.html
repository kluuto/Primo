    Brand challenges

    A brand funds the prize. Everyone who joins races at the same time, but each runner gets a different route of about the same distance and difficulty, so no street gets crowded and nobody can scout the course. Finish times are normalized to 3.00 miles and the fastest wins.

    Run a challenge for your brand

    • You fund the MON prize pool and pick the finish line or checkpoint.
    • Runners get personalized routes that pass your store or café.
    • You get a results report: runners reached, visits, and sign-ups.
